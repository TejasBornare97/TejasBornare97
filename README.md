## Tejas Bornare

I work in claims at AXA UK and build data and AI tools around the problems I see in insurance: long policy wordings, unstructured claims documents, and pricing and retention data. MSc Business Analytics and Management, University of East Anglia.

At the moment I'm focused on retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over insurance documents, and on understanding how language models work underneath by building a small GPT from scratch.

### Projects

**[insurance-rag-assistant](https://github.com/TejasBornare97/insurance-rag-assistant)**
Answers plain-English questions about UK policy wordings and shows the cited passage highlighted on the original PDF page. Hybrid vector and BM25 retrieval, a local Llama 3.1 8B model, and a check in code that flags any figure the cited sources don't contain. 27 of 27 answerable test questions correct, and both unanswerable ones correctly referred.
`Python` `ChromaDB` `sentence-transformers` `Ollama` `Streamlit`

**[Motor insurance renewal modelling](https://github.com/TejasBornare97?tab=repositories&q=motor)** (MSc dissertation)
Compared logistic regression, decision trees and random forest for predicting which motor policies renew, and what drives retention.
`R` `classification` `model evaluation`

### Tools

Python, SQL, R, Power BI, Azure. For RAG work: PyMuPDF, sentence-transformers, ChromaDB, rank-bm25, Ollama, Streamlit.
